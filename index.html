Welcome. This is a research demo of an auto-proofreading system developed by Fuming Yang and Sven Dorkenwald. Inference is performed end-to-end on a complete neuron from the MICrONS dataset in a single pass (~45 seconds). Ground-truth merge regions are pre-marked in a separate colour to aid comparison, but please note that this annotation is not exhaustive — regions outside the GT may still contain genuine merges, so please cross-reference the underlying EM imagery and the segmentation when making your judgement rather than relying on the GT colour alone.

Reviewers step through the suspect windows in the Decision panel and answer two questions per window:

  1. Real merge? — does this location actually contain a merge error in the underlying segmentation?
  2. Split OK? — does the proposed cut (visualised as same-coloured token clusters in the EM viewer, following CAVE's split convention) correctly separate at least one of the merging fragments?

Mark Split = YES whenever the recommended grouping — if applied as a CAVE split — would correctly detach at least one fragment, and NO if it would not yield a valid separation. Decisions persist in your browser's local storage and can be downloaded as JSON via Export decisions when you are finished. We greatly appreciate your contribution!

Keyboard: Y/N/S/U — Real merge?; 1/2/3 — Split OK? (does this cut separate ≥1 merge error?); →/Enter — next undecided; ← — step back one window; ↑/↓ or j/k — adjacent navigation.
